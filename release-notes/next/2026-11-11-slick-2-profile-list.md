- **Switch profile from the picture on Home.** Point at your profile picture at the top right (or click it, or move to it with the keys or the controller, one step past the last button) and a list drops down under it: the other profiles with their picture and name, AnyTogether's mark on the ones linked to an account, and Add profile at the end. Choosing one plays as that profile at once, with the same rules as the "Who's playing?" screen (not while a game runs or saves sync). Escape, Back, moving the pointer away or a click elsewhere closes it. Suggested by Slick Daddy.
