- **PS1 games in the launcher's window can now choose their own resolution.** The Internal resolution row on the game menu's Graphics page has a new first value, Auto, which shows the resolution it picked, for example "Auto (2x)". A game you have not set a resolution for starts on Auto at 2x. While you play, Auto raises the resolution one step after a minute with plenty of spare power, up to what your screen can show. If the game slows down, it lowers the resolution within half a minute. Auto remembers each game's resolution and never goes back to one that ran slow, until you change your graphics card or the game's other graphics settings, or the emulator is updated. Picking a value yourself turns Auto off and keeps that value, as before. It needs an emulator version that reports how fast games run. PS2 games keep the resolution you pick, because they only change resolution when the game restarts.
