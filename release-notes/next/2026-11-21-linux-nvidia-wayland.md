- **Linux with an NVIDIA driver under Wayland: the launcher no longer freezes on an old picture.** After the window went full screen or changed size, the home screen could stay frozen on an earlier frame or the launcher could close. The window now runs through XWayland there, as it does when you set `GDK_BACKEND=x11` yourself, while games keep their own setting. `ANYPS5_NATIVE_WAYLAND=1` keeps native Wayland. `ANYPS5_IMPELLER=0` switches the launcher from Flutter's Impeller renderer to Skia, for a driver that has trouble with it.
