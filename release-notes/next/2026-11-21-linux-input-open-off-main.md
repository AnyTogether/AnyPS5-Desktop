- **Linux: a device that hangs when opened no longer freezes the launcher.** On some HP laptops (OMEN and others with the `hp_accel` freefall sensor) the accelerometer shows up as a joystick, and opening it can hang inside the kernel for good. The launcher opened controller devices on its interface thread, so the whole window froze, including a "convert for this computer" in progress. Controllers are now looked for and opened in the background, accelerometers and other devices that are no controller are left alone, and a device that does not answer is skipped instead of tried again. If SDL2 gets stuck that way, the launcher switches to reading controllers directly. Reported by QguAr71.
