- **Now playing in the quick menu** (asked for by Slick Daddy), as on the Mac. While a music or video player plays or is paused, the menu over a game shows its title, artist, player and cover, with previous, play or pause and next: left, right and ✕ on the controller, the arrow keys and Enter, or the mouse. It reads Windows' media session and MPRIS on Linux, so it works with any player that tells the system what it plays and needs no account. Settings › Behaviour has its switch, and a second one, off until you turn it on, that shows your AnyTogether friends what you are listening to. The player is read only while the menu is open or while friends are shown it.
