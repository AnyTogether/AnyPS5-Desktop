- **Delete game.** Game Settings has a new action under Remove installation that takes the game itself off the disk: its own folder, the update and add-ons dumped beside it, and the zip or .ffpfsc it was unpacked from go to the Recycle Bin (the trash on Linux), and the installation AnyPS5 made from them is removed. The card names the folder and its size and starts on Cancel. Your saves and trophies are kept and come back when you add the game to the same folder again. Backups, cloud saves, captures and your AnyTogether profile are not touched. Where the files cannot go to the Recycle Bin (a drive without one, a folder too big for it), AnyPS5 asks again before deleting them for good. It never deletes a games folder itself, a game reached through a link that leads out of your games folders, or a game that is playing or being unlocked.
