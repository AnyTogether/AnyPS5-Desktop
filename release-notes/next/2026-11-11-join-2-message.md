- **A join that cannot go on says why in full**. A different game version or engine build opens a panel that names which of the two differs and shows the host's and yours whole, each on its own line (before: a one-line notice with the builds cut to six characters, such as "arm-ni" and "arm-f7"). It offers what fits: Rebuild and join when only your copy is stale, Check for updates when your release is the older one, Try anyway for a game version, and it no longer says "update" to the side that is already newer.
