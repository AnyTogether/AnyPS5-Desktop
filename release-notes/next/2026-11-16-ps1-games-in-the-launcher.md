- **PS1 games can play in the launcher's own window.** Once the PS1 emulator is downloaded in Settings › Emulators, a new switch shows beside it, Run PS1 games in the separate host, and the first successful download turns it on. Then a PS1 disc from the emulator library plays inside the launcher through the AnyPS5 emulator host and SwanStation, and DuckStation is not needed. The picture keeps the game's shape, and the game pauses while the launcher's window is not in front. Two controllers play ports 1 and 2, the keyboard plays port 1, and each controller vibrates for its own port. The PS button opens the game's menu: Resume, the discs of a multi-disc game, Audio (volume and mute), Controller, Console and Advanced, then Close game. A PS1 BIOS goes in the folder a new row in Settings › Emulators opens, and a game that cannot start says why (no BIOS, a disc that cannot be read, an emulator that stopped). Screenshots, clips and Remote Play's phone pad do not cover these games yet. With the switch off, or before the emulator is downloaded, PS1 discs start in DuckStation as before.
