- **The LIVE badge on Linux (X11)**, as on the Mac and Windows: while you broadcast, the small badge shows over the game and viewers do not see it. The broadcast now records the game's window by itself (x11grab's window_id, ffmpeg 4.4 or later) instead of its area of the screen, so under a compositor nothing drawn over the game is in it. The badge needs a compositor and stays hidden while the replay buffer or Remote Play records the screen. Under Wayland it stays off for now.
