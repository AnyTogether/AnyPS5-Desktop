- **Your own relinker options and launch options per game** (asked for by Hog). A game's Advanced settings have Custom relinker options under Rebuild options, added after the launcher's own on the next rebuild (Unlock again), and Launch options in a new Launch section: variables as NAME=value first, then arguments for the game, as in `APS5_REPORT=0 --aps5-report=off`. A variable the launcher already sets keeps the launcher's value, and the game's log says which ones. Every keystroke is checked: an option the launcher sets itself, one that would break the rebuilt game, one the relinker does not have, or a quote left open is not saved and the row says why. A relinker that stops with your options rebuilds the game without them.
