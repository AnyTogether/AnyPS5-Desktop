- **Cards over the game under Wayland on KDE Plasma, Sway, Hyprland and the other desktops with wlr-layer-shell**, as on the Mac: trophies and notices while you play are a card over the game instead of a system notice, and the LIVE badge shows while you broadcast (the desktop shares the game's window alone, so viewers do not see it). It needs gtk-layer-shell (libgtk-layer-shell0 on Ubuntu and Debian, which the .deb recommends). GNOME has no such layer and keeps the system notice.
