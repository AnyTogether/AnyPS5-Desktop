- **Linux uses the system's libmpv for clips and voice notes**: the packages no longer carry a libmpv and the 129 libraries it pulled in, so the .deb, the AppImage and the .tar.gz are much smaller. The .deb brings libmpv from apt (`libmpv2`, or `libmpv1` on Ubuntu 22.04), and the Arch package depends on `mpv`. With the AppImage or the .tar.gz on a system without libmpv the launcher still starts and works: a clip shows its picture with the command that installs libmpv on your distribution and a button that opens it in another player, and voice notes open in the system's player.
