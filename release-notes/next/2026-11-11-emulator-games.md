- **PS1, PS2 and PS3 games in an emulator you installed.** Settings › Game Library has a new switch, Emulator games, off until you turn it on: then the library also lists the discs in your games folders (PS1 cue sheets, PBP files and M3U playlists, PS2 ISOs, CHDs in a folder named PS1 or PS2, extracted PS3 discs, and game folders described by a retro.json), and Settings › Emulators appears, where you pick DuckStation, PCSX2 or RPCS3. Play starts the game in that emulator, in its own window. A game whose emulator is not picked yet says Emulator required, and Show as folders gathers each console's games in one tile on the home screen. With the switch off, the games folders are scanned as before and nothing of this shows.
