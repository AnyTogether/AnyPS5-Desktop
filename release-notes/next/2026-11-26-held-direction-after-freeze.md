- **A tap on a direction no longer skips a game when the launcher freezes for a moment.** If the interface stalled right after the press (for example while the sound engine or a game's music was loading in the first seconds after start), the key's release stayed queued behind the freeze, the hold's repeat timer ran out first and moved the selection once more, so Game 1 jumped to Game 3. A repeat that fires long after it was due is now dropped and the wait starts over, for the keyboard and the controller alike; a direction really still held goes on repeating.
