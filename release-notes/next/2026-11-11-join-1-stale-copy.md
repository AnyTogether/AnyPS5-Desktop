- **A game made with another engine is rebuilt before it starts**. After an engine update (or another engine chosen), a game unlocked with the old one was offered "Update and play or Play now", and Play Together and a friend's game started it as it was, so two up-to-date launchers could end up with different builds that cannot connect. Play, Play Together and Join now rebuild the copy first with the usual progress, then go on. A newer engine to download is still offered, and after a rebuild that fails the old copy can still be played as it is.
