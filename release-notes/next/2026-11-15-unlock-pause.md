- **Pause, resume and cancel an unlock.** While a game is being unlocked its button says Pause: the relinker stops where it is and the next game in line starts, and Resume goes on from there (it waits its turn when another unlock is running). The small ✕ beside it cancels the unlock and asks first when work would be lost. Closing the launcher while games are being unlocked asks whether to go back or cancel and quit, so no paused unlock is left behind. Thanks to Lenny4250gh, who built this.
