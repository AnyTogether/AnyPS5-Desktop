- **Remove installation keeps your saves.** A game's saves and trophies live inside the installation AnyPS5 builds, and Remove installation used to delete them with it without saying so. It now keeps them, together with the game's download data, in the games folder under `.AnyPS5/Saves`, and they come back when you unlock the game again, the same way as after Delete game. The card says they are kept. When they cannot be moved aside the installation stays as it is and AnyPS5 says why. Saves are also safe when an unlock is interrupted or fails just as the new installation takes the old one's place: they go back into the game or wait for the next unlock.
