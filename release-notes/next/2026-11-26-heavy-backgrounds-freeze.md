- **Selecting a game with a 4K background no longer freezes the launcher for about a second.** A dump's `pic0.png` is often 3840x2160 (up to 15 MB), and drawing it for the first time held the graphics thread for nearly a second (about 850 ms in a frame log, against 15 to 20 ms normally), also when it was only the next game's picture being prepared. The launcher now makes one lighter copy of each background (1920 px wide, 8 bits) in the background, once, in its cache folder (`Cache\Artwork` on Windows, `~/.cache/AnyPS5/Artwork` on Linux), and draws that instead; the original files are never touched. A background fades in when its copy is ready, and a picture the launcher cannot read is drawn as before.
